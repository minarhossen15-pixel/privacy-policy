Privacy Policy

Pregnancy Guide App · Effective: June 1, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

✅

Most Of Your Data Stays On Your Device

Your pregnancy tracking, diary, medicines, and weight logs are saved locally and never leave your phone. Only the optional Community feature stores a small amount of data securely online, as explained below.

📱 1. Information We Collect

The following data is stored only on your device using local storage, and is never uploaded anywhere:

  • Pregnancy start date & due date
  • Diary entries & mood logs
  • Medicine reminder names & times
  • Weight tracking entries
  • Doctor notes & appointments
  • Optional PIN lock preference

If you choose to create a Community account, we additionally collect the information listed in Section 3 below.

🚫 2. What We Do NOT Do

  • Your pregnancy dates, diary, medicines, weight, and doctor notes never leave your device
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever
  • Account sign-in is only needed if you choose to use Community — everything else works without any login
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts
  • We do not use cookies or third-party analytics trackers
  • We never show your email address to other Community members

💬 3. Community Feature (Optional)

The Community tab lets you connect with other moms — sharing posts, commenting, and sending private messages. It is completely optional and only activates if you choose to sign up or log in.

To use Community, we collect:

  • The display name you choose (shown to other members)
  • Your email address (used only for login — never shown publicly)
  • Posts, comments, replies, and likes you choose to share
  • Private direct messages you send to other members
  • Online / last-active status, shown to people you message

Where it's stored: Community data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Firebase Authentication), hosted on Google Cloud. Access is restricted by security rules so that only you can edit your profile, and only the two people in a conversation can read their private messages.

Firebase Privacy & Security: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal or medical details in public posts, since anything you post is visible to other Community members.

📢 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect non-personal device information (such as device type, OS version, and general ad interaction data) to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

You may opt out of personalized ads via your device's Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

🔔 5. Notifications

This app sends two kinds of notifications:

  • Local reminders (medicine times, daily pregnancy tips) — generated entirely on your device, never touching any server.
  • Push notifications (app updates, and Community activity such as a new message) — delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), which uses a device token solely to deliver the notification to your phone.

You can disable either type at any time from your Android system Settings, or from More → Notifications inside the app.

🗑️ 6. Data Deletion

There are two separate ways to delete your data:

  • On-device data (pregnancy tracker, diary, medicines, weight, notes): go to App Settings → Reset App. Uninstalling the app also deletes this permanently.
  • Community account (profile, posts, messages): go to Community → Account Settings → Delete Account. This is separate from resetting the app — simply uninstalling the app will not delete your Community data.

⚕️ 7. Medical Disclaimer

This app is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a medical device, not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Content shared by other users in the Community feature reflects their personal experience only and is not medical advice. Always consult a licensed physician or qualified healthcare provider regarding any health concerns during pregnancy.

👶 8. Children's Privacy

This app, including the Community feature, is designed for use by adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has created an account or provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

📄 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, when we add new features like Community. Any changes will be reflected on this page, along with an updated "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
