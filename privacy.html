Privacy Policy

Pregnancy guide App · Effective: June 1, 2026

✅
Your Data Stays On Your Device

Pregnancy Companion does not collect, upload, or share any of your personal data. Everything is saved locally on your phone only.

📱
1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Pregnancy start date & due date
  • Diary entries & mood logs
  • Medicine reminder names & times
  • Weight tracking entries
  • Doctor notes & appointments
  • Optional PIN lock preference
🚫
2. What We Do NOT Do
  • We do not send your data to any server
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties
  • We do not require account registration or login
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts
  • We do not use cookies or analytics trackers
📢
3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect non-personal device information to serve relevant advertisements. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

📌 Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

🔔
4. Notifications

This app sends local notifications only — for medicine reminders and daily health tips. These are generated on your device and are not connected to any server. You may disable notifications at any time via Android Settings.

🗑️
5. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time via App Settings → Reset App. Uninstalling the app will also delete all data permanently.

⚕️
6. Medical Disclaimer

This app is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a licensed physician regarding any health concerns during pregnancy.

👶
7. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

📧
8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store page of Pregnancy Companion.
